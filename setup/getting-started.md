# Getting Started

*The first thing to read. This guide figures out where you are and takes you down the right path.*

---

## Before You Start

You need a **Claude Pro or Max subscription** ($20-100/month at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)). The free tier won't work with Claude Code. If you're not sure which plan you're on, check [claude.ai/settings](https://claude.ai/settings).

---

## Which Best Describes You?

### Path A: "I've never opened a terminal on my computer"

No problem. You don't need to be technical to use this - but you do need to get a few things installed first. The good news: Claude can walk you through every step.

**Go to:** [Non-technical setup guide](non-technical-setup.md)

**Time:** About 30-45 minutes for the first-time setup. After that, it just works.

---

### Path B: "I can follow terminal instructions and I'm comfortable copy-pasting commands"

You don't need to be a developer. If you can open a terminal, paste a command, and hit enter, you'll be fine.

**Go to:** [SETUP.md](../SETUP.md) for the guided setup, then come back here for the advanced features when you're ready.

**Time:** About 15-20 minutes.

---

### Path C: "I'm a developer / I know what I'm doing"

You probably don't need the guides. Read the [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) to understand the system design, clone the repo, edit the files directly, and you'll be up and running in minutes.

The setup guides in this folder are here if you want them:
- [Telegram - chat from your phone](telegram-setup.md)
- [Wiki memory - persistent memory](wiki-memory-setup.md)
- [Scheduled agents - email alerts and meeting prep](scheduled-agents-setup.md)
- [Content scanning - YouTube, newsletters, RSS](content-scanning-setup.md)

**Time:** However long you want to spend customising.

---

## After Initial Setup

Once you've got the basics running (CLAUDE.md personalised, morning check-in working), here's the order I'd recommend adding things:

| Step | What | Why | Guide |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | **Wiki memory** | Gives your Chief of Staff a memory. Without it, every session starts blank. | [Setup guide](wiki-memory-setup.md) |
| 2 | **Telegram** | Chat from your phone. Captures ideas on the go, checks your calendar between meetings. | [Setup guide](telegram-setup.md) |
| 3 | **Scheduled agents** | Email alerts and meeting prep briefs, automatically. This is where it starts feeling like a real assistant. | [Setup guide](scheduled-agents-setup.md) |
| 4 | **Content scanning** | Automated monitoring of your industry sources. Optional but powerful if you follow a lot of content. | [Setup guide](content-scanning-setup.md) |

You don't need all of these. Start with what solves your most immediate problem and add the rest when you're ready.

## If You Get Stuck

At any point during setup, if you hit something you don't understand:

1. **Ask Claude.** Paste the instruction you're stuck on into Claude and say "I don't understand this - can you walk me through it step by step?" Claude is very good at translating technical instructions into plain English.

2. **Check the troubleshooting section** at the bottom of each guide.

3. **Ask for help.** If you're stuck and frustrated, [get in touch](mailto:hamish@goagentflow.com). We built this system and we're happy to help. If you want us to set the whole thing up for you, that's something [AgentFlow](https://goagentflow.com) can do too.
