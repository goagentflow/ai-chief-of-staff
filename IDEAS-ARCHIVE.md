## How to Use This File

**Move ideas here when:**

- You've decided not to pursue them (capture why for future reference)
- They're not relevant now but might be later

- You might change your mind later
- Context helps you remember why you said no
- Patterns emerge (what kinds of ideas do you keep dismissing?)
