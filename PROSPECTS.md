## How to Use This File

**Add prospects when:**
- New business enquiry comes in
- Networking leads to a potential opportunity
- Someone expresses interest in working together

**Update after:**
- Every interaction (call, email, meeting)
- Status changes (proposal sent, waiting for X, deal won/lost)
- New information about their needs or timeline

**Move to CLIENTS.md when:**
- Deal is won and work begins
- They become an active client (not just a prospect)

**Archive when:**
- Deal is lost or goes cold
- Move to bottom of this file under "## Archived" with a note on what happened
